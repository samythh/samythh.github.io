Featured Award

PERISAI — 2nd Place, Core3D Hackathon 2026.

2nd Place · May 2026

Protecting children from online gambling with AI.

In an intensive 24-hour hackathon held by the Computer Engineering Student Association of Universitas Andalas, my team co-developed PERISAI — an AI-powered parental control app that shields children from online gambling exposure.

  • Real-time detection using a CNN model that analyses on-screen content and extracts text accurately.
  • Automated monitoring that sends alert notifications with screenshot evidence to parents' devices when illicit content is detected.
  • Designed, built, and demonstrated a fully functional prototype within the strict 24-hour constraint.
Watch the PERISAI demo

Process — built in 24 hours

  1. 01 Ideate

    Framing the problem

    Spent the first hours scoping children's exposure to online gambling and what a parent actually needs to see.

  2. 02 Model

    Real-time detection

    Built detection on a CNN that reads on-screen content and extracts text to flag illicit material.

  3. 03 Integrate

    Alert pipeline

    Wired detection to an alert flow that pushes a notification with screenshot evidence to the parent's device.

  4. 04 Pitch

    Demo against the clock

    Packaged a fully working prototype and pitched it — landing 2nd place within the 24-hour limit.
